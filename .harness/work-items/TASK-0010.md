1. `plan activate` and `task add` express intent rather than record fields, and
   maintain both sides of the PLAN/TASK link themselves.
2. `next` reports the single next step an agent should take from current state.
3. `new` never emits a record that violates its own schema, and derived
   `approval_dependencies` are maintained by the harness rather than by hand.
